Brick Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2026

Brick is a home browser. It doesn't want your data, and it collects as little as it can.

What leaves your device

Search. The city you search is sent to the map and listing providers that answer it.

Your account, on the website only. If you sign in on the website, your sign-in and saved homes are stored in our database on Cloudflare. You can ask us to delete your account and everything in it. The iPhone app has no accounts. Homes you save there stay on your device.

Photos. Listing photos are resized by wsrv.nl, an image service, so they load fast. It sees which photo is being shown, not who you are.

Site analytics. The website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies and without tracking you across sites. The iPhone app has no analytics.

What we never do

No ads. No selling data. No tracking cookies.

Kids

Brick isn't aimed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anything from them.
