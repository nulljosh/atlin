# Brick Privacy Policy

*Last updated September 2026*

Brick is a home browser. It doesn't want your data, and it collects as little as it can.

## What leaves your device
- **Your account.** Sign-in and the things you post are stored in our database on Cloudflare (D1). You can ask us to delete your account and everything in it. This only applies if you sign in.
- **Search.** Places you search are sent to the map and listing providers that answer them.
- **Site analytics.** The website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies and without tracking you across sites.

## What we never do
No ads. No selling data. No tracking cookies.

## Kids
Brick isn't aimed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anything from them.
